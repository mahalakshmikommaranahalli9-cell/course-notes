🧠 Recommended Study Flow

  • Start with the basic definitions.
  • Understand the transition diagrams.
  • Practice DFA construction problems.
  • Practice NFA → DFA conversion.
  • Work through numerical/problem-based questions.
  • Revise important 2, 5 and 10-mark questions.
  • Use the quick-revision sections before examinations.

🎯 Exam Preparation

  • Learn definitions accurately.
  • Practice drawing automata neatly.
  • Show transition tables wherever useful.
  • For conversion problems, show every intermediate step.
  • Clearly mark initial and final states.
  • Write the final conclusion for proof/construction problems.
